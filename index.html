wordpass v1.7.0 · 2026-11-19 10:29 EST

Nothing leaves your browser. Passwords are made with crypto.getRandomValues, are never stored, and the app works offline once loaded. On iPhone or iPad, use Share → Add to Home Screen to install it.

Presets are saved only on this device. Apple has no public link for opening Passwords. To get an “Open Passwords app” link, create a Shortcut named Open Passwords with the Open App action, then tap Enable below.

  • Words: EFF Large and Short Wordlists (CC BY 3.0 US), plus a Filipino list of food and everyday words
